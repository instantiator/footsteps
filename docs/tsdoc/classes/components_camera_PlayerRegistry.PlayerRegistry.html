    Class PlayerRegistry

    Follows what has happened to each player across frames, for longer than the tracker follows the people themselves.

    Player state is kept by a stable id, as a tracker's ids restart with each tracking loop. When the tracker loses a player who isn't active, they stay as a note of where they were, which a new person appearing near there inherits: a removed player the model loses for a moment shouldn't come back as a new, active one. Someone who walks out of shot and back as anyone else's replacement isn't told apart; see docs/player-tracking.md.
