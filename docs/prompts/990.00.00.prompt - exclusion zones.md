Track the intention to remove things that look like people but aren't (a TV, a mirror, a poster) without ever cutting a real player. This prompt is a placeholder: it isn't ready to plan yet.

# Context

A TV showing the game, a mirror or a poster of people produces visual artefacts and stray "people" in the cut-out. The TV is usually above and behind the floor, so its "people" are already treated as spectators. Zones would still matter for what they draw and cost, and for mirrors or posters near the floor's edge.

The plan [003.00.03](<003.00.03.plan - behaviour and scale fixes.md>) (option D7) sketched the design. It builds it only if the first camera session shows zone-shaped artefacts are a key factor in the spectator problem.

# Key constraint

**A player on the game floor must never have their pixels zeroed, even when standing in front of a zone.** Only images coming from the TV, mirror or poster are removed. A mistake that cuts a player is worse than doing nothing.

That rules out removing pixels from the model's input, or from every mask, because both would cut a player standing in front of the TV.

# Intended design

Apply zones per person, after tracking and the spectator decision:

- **Players keep everything:** a person whose ground point is on the floor keeps all their pixels, including those inside zones.
- **Images are dropped whole:** a person whose mask lies mostly inside a zone, with no on-floor ground point, is dropped entirely, as an image rather than a person.
- **Strays are dropped:** any person's pixels inside a zone that aren't connected to their main body are dropped. [`MaskComponents.ts`](../../src/vision/MaskComponents.ts) already finds the main body and removes strays, so zones would extend it.
- **Calibration page:** the user marks rectangles over the offending things.
- **Storage:** zones are kept per camera, alongside the floor calibration ([`FloorCalibration.ts`](../../src/components/camera/FloorCalibration.ts)).
- **Mirrors:** a mirror beside the floor reflects the player, but the reflection's ground point is off the floor, so it is dropped. That is the intended result, and the hard case to test.

# Questions to answer when planning

- **Evidence:** do zone-shaped artefacts matter enough, after the 003.00.03 fixes, to justify new UI and storage?
- **"Mostly inside":** what share of a mask inside a zone drops the person, and how to avoid dropping a player whose ground point is briefly lost.
- **Shapes:** are rectangles enough, or do mirrors and posters at an angle need polygons?
- **Calibration:** how the user marks zones on the live view, and how they are shown afterwards.
- **Storage:** how zones are keyed to a camera and configuration, and what happens when the camera moves.
- **Cost:** whether the per-person check is cheap enough at the frame rates in [ADR 0005](../ADRs/0005-vision-models.md).
