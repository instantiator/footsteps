## Jumping

**Tested with: the floor is lava.** Jumping is not successfully detected. I suspect a combination of body segmentation lag and low framerate mean it's not possible properly detect a player making a small jump. These happen very quickly, and it's difficult to identify the difference between walking around and jumping anyway.

We should alter the demo game, and write some prompts to improve jump detection as future work.

### Workaround: modify the floor is lava

Instead of 3 stones, the player should have a curved bridge that runs from the start position to the end. The goal being to walk along it carefully, rather than use jumps.

We can rename it 'Bridge across lava'.

![floor is lava replacement suggestion](./illustrations/003.00.00%20-%20floor%20is%20lava%20suggestion.png)

The picture is illustrative. It doesn't show perspective applied to the floor texture (and also the floor texture's license isn't checked, so it's probably not appropriate to use - so find an alternative or gradient). Similarly, the wall texture at the back probably isn't ideal either. The goal is to produce the effect of a large cavern with a bridge across it that the player can walk along.

- At the back of the bridge is a black exit door - reaching the door completes the challenge
- Stepping outside the bridge (ie. onto any other part of the floor, which is lava) initiates this sequence:
  1. the player gets static for 2 seconds, with a red glow (ie. a visible warning)
  2. if the player is still standing on lava after 2 seconds
     - the player falls (static and glow remains)
     - the challenge then resets
- The bridge is broken into 5 sections
  - When the player passes from a section to the next, the section they have left follows this pattern:
    1. quakes for a second
    2. quakes and falls in parallel

## Add a ring collection challenge/stage

The ring collection game is new. We can think of it like a bonus level. The goal is to pick up as many rings as possible in 30 seconds.

![ring collection game suggestion](./illustrations/003.00.00%20-%20ring%20collection%20demo%20game%20suggestion.png)

Player must start in the start position.

Rings appear one at a time in a random position, and the player just needs to stand near a ring or touch with a hand to collect it. When they do their count increases, the ring shows a reward, flies off the screen, and a new ring appears in a random position - the player must then collect the next ring.

Not shown in the image: Rings show as hovering above the playing floor, probably a smidge smaller than shown in the illustration. They should be gently floating with a small vertical motion. They can occasionally sparkle.

At 30 seconds, the remaining ring disappears, and the player's current score is shown in large for 10s, more centrally on the screen. Then the scene completes.
