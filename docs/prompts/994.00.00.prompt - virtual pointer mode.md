Track the intention to let camera players use dialogs, scrolls and other pointer controls with their hands. This prompt is a placeholder: it isn't ready to plan yet.

# Context

Dialogs and scrolls are made for the mouse: their pages turn with arrow buttons, and some ask for typed answers. In camera mode, a player standing in front of the screen can't reach them. [002.03.10](<002.03.10.prompt - new games and badges.md>) found a player's cut-out drawn between a scroll's paper and its text. It fixed this by drawing a dialog whole, in front of every player, because only the mouse uses dialogs today.

This prompt would add a way for a camera player to point at and press things, so a game could keep the player in camera mode through its dialogs.

# Intended mode

A **virtual pointer** avatar mode (or a setting on `camera`), for scenes that hold pointer controls:

- **Tracking:** follows one player's hand relative to their head, so a small, comfortable movement covers the whole screen. Raising or pushing the hand forward could act as a press.
- **Pointer:** maps that hand position to a virtual pointer on screen and draws it with a clear graphic. The engine supplies a default, and a game can replace it with its own resource.
- **Events:** the pointer fires the same interactions as the mouse (`mouse-enter`, `mouse-exit`, `click`), so existing rules, dialogs and scrolls work unchanged.

# Questions to answer when planning

- **Who drives it:** with several players, which one's hand drives the pointer. The nearest player, the first to raise a hand, or each player with a pointer of their own?
- **Pressing:** what counts as a press, and how to avoid accidental ones. A dwell time, as `holdMs` does, a push towards the camera, or a closed hand? BodyPix gives hand regions, not finger poses.
- **Smoothing:** how much smoothing the hand position needs to be steady enough to aim at an arrow button, at the frame rates in [ADR 0005](../ADRs/0005-vision-models.md).
- **Typed answers:** how dialogs that ask for one would work. An on-screen keyboard, multiple choice, or speech?
- **Depth:** whether dialogs should then sit among the players by depth again, or stay in front of them.
