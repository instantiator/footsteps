## Decisions made during planning and review

Made while planning, and after reviewing the pages in a browser. They take precedence over the mock-up above.

### Calibration page

- **Two columns:** the title and instructions on the left, about a third of the width, and the camera calibration
  block on the right. The mock-up shows them stacked.
- **Clear all calibrations stays:** "Forget all saved areas" is kept, renamed **Clear all calibrations**, in the
  bottom bar after Reset.

### Landing page

- **Clear all calibrations** is added too, in the bottom bar next to Plan space, for games that use the floor.
- **Floor buttons:** Reset, Clear and Calibrate, in that order, to the right of the camera preview (under it on a
  narrow screen).
- **Back to games:** a button at the far left of the bottom bar.
- **Space:** the camera configuration panel takes up the space available to it, and the camera preview is as large
  as it can be in that space, keeping its aspect.
- **Mirroring:** "Mirror picture" starts off, as the default camera is behind the player, not facing them.

### Home page

- **Setup and design:** a heading below the local repository's game tiles, with **Plan your space** and then a new
  **Scene designer** link (to `/scene-renderer`) under it.
- **Dependencies:** the dependency list moves to its own page, `/dependencies`. It is linked directly under the
  Attributions heading, before each game's attributions.
- **Base path:** the site's base path, shown under the title, now appears only in debug mode (`?debug=true`), as
  "Base path: …".

### Both pages

- **Shared parts:** the parts the landing and calibration pages share are reusable components: the bottom button
  bar, the Plan space link, the warnings over the picture, and Clear all calibrations.
