Track the work of finding out how to detect small, quick jumps reliably. This prompt is a placeholder: it isn't ready to plan yet.

# Context

The floor-is-lava stage asked players to jump between stones, and jumps were often missed, especially small quick ones. [003.00.00](<003.00.00.prompt - gameplay feedback.md>) replaced it with "Bridge across lava", which is walked rather than jumped. The cause was never established. The suspects are:

- **Segmentation lag:** the cut-out trails the player, so a jump is seen late or blurred.
- **Low frame rate:** a small jump lasts only a few frames ([ADR 0008](../ADRs/0008-jump-detection.md)).
- **Walking looks like jumping:** a step rises and falls like a small jump (see [outstanding issues](../outstanding-issues.md)).

These are suspicions, not findings. [`JumpDetector.ts`](../../src/components/camera/JumpDetector.ts) is the detector in question, and [ADR 0005](../ADRs/0005-vision-models.md) records the models and frame rates in use.

# Intended work

- **A calibration game:** guided prompts ("stand", "walk", "jump", "duck") that label each moment, so every recorded frame has a known truth.
- **A record option:** in `?debug=true`, record camera frames with timestamps and labels to a downloadable file, from one or more testers.
- **A replay bench:** extend [`vision-bench.spec.ts`](../../tests/browser/vision-bench.spec.ts) to replay recordings through candidate models and settings (BodyPix today, MoveNet, BlazePose, several input resolutions). It measures jump precision and recall, detection latency and achieved fps.

# Questions to answer by investigation

- **Frame rate and resolution:** which make a small jump detectable is unknown. It is the bench's main output.
- **Landmarks:** whether to use the feet alone or also the head rising with them ([ADR 0008](../ADRs/0008-jump-detection.md), option 3), and which models place them well enough.
- **Recording format and size:** a video, a frame sequence or key points only, and how large a few minutes per tester becomes.
- **Consent:** how to ask testers before recording them, where recordings are kept, and whether key points alone would do.

# Further BodyPix tuning with real recordings

Stills don't show flicker. Once the record option exists, repeat the sweep from [003.00.03](<003.00.03.plan - behaviour and scale fixes.partial.md>) (option D5) against recorded footage of real players:

- **Model settings:** `bodypixThreshold`, `scoreThreshold` with `nmsRadius`, and `bodypixRefineSteps`.
- **Stray-patch settings:** `visionBlobMinShare` and `visionBlobMaxGap`.
- **Spectator holds:** `spectatorHoldMs` and `spectatorReturnMs`.

Measure, for each setting:

- **Flicker:** spectator flips per minute while a player stands on the floor.
- **Lost limbs:** hands and feet missing from the mask.
- **Frame rate:** the achieved fps.

Set the defaults from the footage, as it may move the values chosen from photos.
