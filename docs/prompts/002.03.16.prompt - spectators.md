Prepare a plan to tell spectators apart from players, so people standing off the floor don't play.

# Context

Everyone the camera sees is a player, including people watching from the side of the room or sitting on a sofa behind the floor. They get a player number, count in `game.players`, fire interactions, appear in position variables, and are drawn in the scene. [002.03.04](<002.03.04.prompt - calibration.md>) and [002.03.06](<002.03.06.prompt - floor placement.md>) gave each player a position on the calibrated floor, which is what makes it possible to tell them apart.

# Spectators

For now, **a spectator is someone outside the floor area**:

- Their held position (`PlayerPositions`, which already holds through jumps) falls outside the floor (x or y outside 0–100) by more than a margin, about 5 floor units.
- They have stayed there for longer than a short hold, so stepping over an edge doesn't drop a player at once, and stepping onto the floor makes someone a player again after the same hold.
- 2D scenes have no floor, so nobody is a spectator there.

Spectators get no player number, interactions, position variables or count. The filter goes between `PlayerPositions` and the player registry and interactions, in `CharacterCanvas`'s loop. Think about what happens to a player's effects (falling, static, frozen) when they step off the floor, and to their number if they step back on.

# How spectators are shown

A game chooses how spectators are drawn, with a setting that a stage or scene can override, following the same inheritance as `modules.scene` and `modules.avatar`:

| Option            | Spectators are                                                                                                                |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hidden (default)  | Not drawn at all                                                                                                              |
| Shown and ignored | Drawn in the scene, but can't interact, with an optional look: an opacity, a CSS filter, or an effect such as `player-static` |

- **Strength by proximity (optional):** the look's strength can follow how far the spectator is from the floor. Someone just off its edge is nearly solid; someone at the back of the room is faint or fully static. Work out how distance maps to strength (eg. a range in floor units), and how it eases.
- Show and ignore works with the debug view: spectators should be marked as such.
- Consider whether `game.players` should report spectators separately (eg. `game.players.spectators`), so a scene can say "3 people watching".
