## Context

Manual testing found five problems:

- Jumps can't be detected reliably, so floor-is-lava can't be won.
- Reset scenes look inconsistent.
- The pits are too shallow.
- The effects stage is flat 2D.
- In jump and duck, the mouse misses the first obstacles.

The prompt also asks for:

- a ring-collection bonus stage;
- one demo game that holds every test and demo scene;
- a future-work prompt on improving jump detection.

### Constraints and reuse

- **Per-stage 2D/2.5D:** a scene's or reset scene's `scene` field (`src/entities/data/scenes/GameSceneData.ts:83,97`) overrides the game's `modules.scene`.
- **Effects to reuse** are listed in `src/entities/data/scenes/GameEffectData.ts`:
  - `quake`, `item-fall`, `item-move` and `highlight` (`src/components/core/effects/ItemEffects.ts`);
  - `player-static`, `player-fall` and `player-spin-away` (`PlayerEffects.ts:242`);
  - `stop`.
- **Triggers to reuse:** `every`, `after`, `when` + `forMs`, `effectEnd`, `holdMs`, `series`/`parallel`, and `random(min, max)` (`src/components/core/logic/GameExpressionUtils.ts`).
- **Floor placement:**
  - `data-floor-area`, and `data-floor-x/y/height/anchor/thickness` (`src/components/core/layout/FloorPlacement.ts`);
  - surface classes `screen-background`, `rear-background` and `floor-background`;
  - `var(--floor-unit)` (`docs/positions-sizes-units.md`).
- **Covered lava:** `foot-enter` on a `floor-background` doesn't fire through floor areas covering it (`includeCovered` defaults to false), so bridge sections make safe ground.
- **Mouse-feet:**
  - It fires only `foot-*`, `any-*`, jumps and pointer events (`AvatarModeUtils.ts:47`), so "near" must also be a `foot-enter` floor area.
  - Player effects do nothing in mouse-feet mode, so any warning must also show in the template.
- **Templates** bind `state.*` and `data.*` through Handlebars, which has no comparisons, so keep flags in state. Decorative items get `pointer-events: none`.
- **Rules:** no `any`; zero lint warnings; comments state intent. `check.sh --fast` runs after every edit through the hook.

**Outcome:**

- The "first two missed" report didn't reproduce in the browser suite, with a parked or a moving pointer. Spawns wait
  for their scene's run to be on screen, ids start at 1 with none lost, and the camera layers let the pointer through.
- The traces found one obstacle costing several lives, as a pointer crossed its edge or the bat's notched outline more
  than once. Obstacles now despawn on a hit, which is a game-data fix.
- An early version of the regression test treated a moment's detachment by idiomorph as the obstacle leaving. The
  final tests tell passing from position.
- The unreproduced report is recorded in `docs/outstanding-issues.md` for the next manual session.
